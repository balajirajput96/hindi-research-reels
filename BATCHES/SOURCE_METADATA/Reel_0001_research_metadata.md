# Reel 0001 — Research Metadata

**Topic:** Practice-dependent neuroplasticity.
**Evidence label:** Scientific review evidence, communicated with explicit limitations.
**Primary reel claim:** Structured skill practice can be associated with functional and structural neural changes; the exact response varies and MRI measures are indirect.

| Claim in production | Evidence classification | Necessary limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Skill learning can be associated with neural adaptation. | Established review evidence. | Results depend on task, dose, development, prior experience, and study design. |
| Brain activity, connectivity, and some structural measures can change with training. | MRI review evidence. | MRI measurements are indirect and do not directly show a single synapse or neuron. |
| Practice, feedback, progressive challenge, and rest can support learning conditions. | Evidence-informed educational framing. | This is not a universal promise, treatment recommendation, or instant transformation claim. |

## Verified sources

1. Tymofiyeva, O., & Gaschler, R. (2021). *Training-Induced Neural Plasticity in Youth: A Systematic Review of Structural and Functional MRI Studies*. Frontiers in Human Neuroscience, 14, 497245. The review included 71 MRI studies and reported training-associated changes while noting control-condition and developmental limitations. [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7848153/).
2. Dayan, E., & Cohen, L. G. (2011). *Neuroplasticity subserving motor skill learning*. Neuron, 72(3), 443–454. The review describes functional and structural plasticity across acquisition, consolidation, and retention of motor skills. [PMC full text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3217208/).
