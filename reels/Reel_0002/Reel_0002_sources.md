# Reel 0002 — Research Sources and Claim Boundaries

## Topic

**नई आदत शुरू करने में “अगर–तब” योजना कैसे मदद कर सकती है?**

## Evidence-safe takeaway

The reel explains that a goal intention alone does not guarantee action. It introduces an implementation intention: a self-selected plan that links a specified realistic situation to a feasible action in an if–then format. The content states that this planning strategy can support action initiation; it does not promise goal attainment, automatic habit formation, brain rewiring, or medical/mental-health treatment.

| Reel statement | Classification | Source support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “सिर्फ इरादा बना लेना, हर बार काम शुरू नहीं कराता।” | Scientific evidence boundary | Strong goal intentions do not guarantee action. [1] [2] |
| “अगर यह स्थिति आए, तब मैं यह छोटा काम करूँगा।” | Scientific evidence | This is the standard if–then structure of an implementation intention. [1] [2] |
| “If-then plans शुरुआत करने में मदद कर सकते हैं।” | Scientific evidence, non-guarantee framing | Reviews and meta-analytic findings support average effects on goal initiation/attainment, but the reel does not translate aggregate findings into an individual promise. [1] [3] |
| Choose a real cue and a small feasible step. | Practical behavioural strategy | The plan should specify a suitable opportunity and a response that addresses the relevant self-regulatory problem. [2] |
