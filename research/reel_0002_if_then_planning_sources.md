# Reel 0002 — Research Notes

## Working topic

**नई आदत शुरू करने में “अगर–तब” योजना कैसे मदद कर सकती है?**

## Unique angle

Reel 0001 corrected the mistaken belief that a habit has a universal 21-day deadline. Reel 0002 addresses a different problem: **a person can intend to start, yet miss the moment to act.** It introduces an implementation intention—an explicit “if situation X happens, then I will do action Y” plan—as a research-backed planning method, not as a promise of automatic habit formation.

## Claim boundaries

| Claim for the reel | Evidence classification | Safe Hindi framing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A goal intention alone does not guarantee action. | Scientific evidence | “सिर्फ इरादा बना लेना, हर बार काम शुरू करवा दे—यह ज़रूरी नहीं।” |
| An implementation intention links a specified cue with a specified action in an if–then format. | Scientific evidence | “अगर यह स्थिति आए, तब मैं यह छोटा काम करूँगा।” |
| Such planning can support translating goals into action and can improve noticing a chosen cue. | Scientific evidence, stated without personal guarantee | “यह योजना सही मौके को पहचानने और शुरुआत करने में मदद कर सकती है।” |
| The chosen cue should be a realistic opportunity and the action should be feasible. | Practical behavioural strategy | “ऐसा संकेत चुनें जो सच में आपके दिन में आता हो, और कदम इतना छोटा हो कि किया जा सके।” |
| An if–then plan does not replace medical care, treatment, or all other behaviour-change supports. | Safety boundary | “यह एक planning tool है, इलाज या जादुई shortcut नहीं।” |

## Source findings

A peer-reviewed review explains implementation intentions as plans that specify **when, where, and how** an action will be performed using an if–then format. The review distinguishes such plans from merely holding a goal intention and describes the proposed processes as greater accessibility of the selected cue and a stronger cue–response link. [1]

The same review reports the 2006 Gollwitzer and Sheeran meta-analysis of 94 independent tests involving more than 8,000 participants. It reported a medium-to-large average effect on goal attainment (d = 0.65). The reel should not present this aggregate effect as a guarantee for an individual, a particular behaviour, or a fixed outcome. [1] [2]

A U.S. National Cancer Institute reference overview similarly defines if–then plans as linking critical situations with goal-directed responses and notes that even strong goal intentions do not ensure goal attainment. That resource is marked as no longer maintained, so it is used here only as a concordant explanatory source alongside the peer-reviewed review and original meta-analysis. [2]

## Reel-specific communication rule

The narration must avoid claims that a cue will force action, that a person can “rewire” their brain instantly, or that an if–then plan guarantees habit formation. The video is educational and should recommend one small, self-chosen, realistic action.
