# Reel 0001 — Research Sources and Claim Boundaries

## Topic

**क्या आदत सच में 21 दिन में बनती है?**

## Evidence-safe takeaway

The reel explains that a universal 21-day habit deadline is not supported as a personal scientific guarantee. It describes habit formation as increasing automaticity through repeated action in a stable context, emphasizes the large variation between people and behaviours, and offers a small self-chosen action linked to a recurring cue as a practical strategy. It does not offer medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or an individual timetable.

| Reel statement | Classification | Source support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “21 दिन वाला नियम वैज्ञानिक deadline नहीं है।” | Scientific evidence boundary | The evidence does not support a universal fixed personal deadline. [1] [3] |
| Repetition in a stable context can increase automaticity. | Scientific evidence | This is the behavioural principle studied in real-world habit-formation research. [1] [2] |
| “66 दिन भी आपकी deadline नहीं है।” | Scientific evidence boundary | The reported average/median estimates and individual differences must not be converted into a personal forecast. [2] [3] |
| Connect a small action to a recurring cue. | Practical behavioural strategy | This is framed as an educational design strategy, not a guarantee or clinical treatment. [1] [2] |
