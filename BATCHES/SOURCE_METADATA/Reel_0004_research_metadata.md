## Evidence-backed statements

The reel explains that choice architecture changes the setting in which options are presented while preserving choice. It identifies changing option-related effort as a decision-structure technique. It frames making a desired action easier and an unwanted action a little harder as a personal experiment, not a promise.

## Limits spoken in the reel

Effects vary across people and situations. The reel does not claim that an environmental tweak is treatment, that it guarantees behavior change, or that effects necessarily persist after the setup is removed.
