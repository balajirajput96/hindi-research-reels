# Reel 0004 evidence record — choice architecture and environmental friction

## Working topic

**Hindi title:** आदत बदलनी है? काम को आसान, रुकावट को थोड़ा कठिन बनाइए

**Concept key:** `choice_architecture_option_effort`

## Evidence classification

| Planned claim | Classification | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| The way options are arranged can influence behavior. | Scientific evidence | Choice-architecture research examines physical, social, and information environments. |
| Changing the effort needed to choose an option is a decision-structure technique. | Scientific evidence | This is a taxonomy-level claim; it does not promise a personal outcome. |
| Choice-architecture interventions show average positive effects across studies. | Scientific evidence with limitation | Mertens et al. report an overall small-to-medium pooled effect, substantial heterogeneity, and publication-bias concerns. The script does not give the pooled number. |
| Prompts and environmental changes may work while present, but effects after removal are less certain. | Scientific evidence with limitation | A physical-activity review found more positive findings during intervention than after removal and did not establish broad durable effects. |
| Put the desired action one step easier and an unwanted action one step harder. | Practical experiment | This is an optional self-observation exercise, not treatment or a guarantee. |

## Script prohibitions

The reel must not claim that friction changes behavior for everyone, that any particular setup is a treatment, that a single nudge creates a lasting habit, or that lack of behavior change is personal failure.
