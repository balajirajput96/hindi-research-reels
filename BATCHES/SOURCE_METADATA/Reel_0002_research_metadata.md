**Topic cluster:** Habits, automaticity, context cues, and behavior change
**Completion status at export:** QC passed; Drive upload pending

## Claim summary

The reel corrects the universal “21-day habit” myth. It explains that habit automaticity timelines vary, that 59–66 day medians in some reviewed studies are not individual guarantees, and that stable contexts were associated with higher automaticity in the cited research.

## Evidence boundary

This is general behavior-science education, not treatment advice. The reel avoids diagnostic, clinical, or outcome guarantees. It states that a missed day can be followed by a small restart rather than presenting streaks as moral success or failure.
