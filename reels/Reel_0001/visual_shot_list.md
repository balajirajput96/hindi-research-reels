## Generation prompt

Create a polished 9:16 vertical editorial explainer animation about habit formation. Use warm indigo, deep teal, and subtle amber highlights; a clean modern 3D illustration style; diverse anonymous Indian-adult silhouettes without recognizable public figures; gentle parallax, camera pushes, object transforms, and soft motion graphics. Depict a calendar changing into a question mark, a toothbrush cue leading to a glass of water, anonymous research-tracker lines that rise and plateau, visibly varied paths, and a calm final habit loop. Keep the lower third uncluttered for externally rendered Hindi captions. Avoid logos, journal covers, medical claims, precise individual timelines, and any “21 days guarantees a habit” message. Cinematic, high-resolution, vertical social-video composition, consistent lighting and design system.

The generated visuals must be 9:16, contain no illegible embedded text, reserve the lower third for captions, and support rather than overstate the sourced claim. If a motion generator is unavailable, do not substitute static text slides and call it AI animation; log the production blocker instead.
