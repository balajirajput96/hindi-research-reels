## Evidence-backed statements

The reel defines retrieval practice as attempting to bring studied information to mind without looking at the source first. It says research syntheses found practice testing can benefit later learning compared with restudying in many studied contexts, while emphasizing that format, feedback, timing, subject matter, and learner/task conditions can affect results.

## Limits spoken in the reel

The reel makes no exam-score guarantee and does not present active recall as a medical treatment. It presents the two-question routine as a personal learning experiment and states that deeper skills also require understanding, practice, and feedback.
